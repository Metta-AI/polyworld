Revised eye atlas: 4 x 4 pairs, 1254 x 1254 pixels.

Use the transparent art plus the Non-Color pupil mask.
White mask pixels select grayscale iris/pupil regions.
Black mask pixels protect white sclera, white highlights, dark accents, and background.

rgb = mix(art.rgb, art.rgb * pupilTint.rgb, mask.r)
alpha = art.a

White tint preserves the grayscale art. Black tint produces black pupils
while leaving the white highlights and sclera unchanged. Multiplication
preserves the iris shading for other tint colors. Do not tint the whole material.

Use the measured contentRect bounds and uvRect cells from atlas.json.
The generated columns are not exactly evenly spaced; do not cut at quarters.
The source alpha is retained. An alpha cutoff of 0.5 suits the game decal material.
