Generated eye atlas v1

16 pairs in a 4 by 4 grid. The PNG is 1254 by 1254 pixels.
Use quarter-width/quarter-height UV cells instead of assuming integer cell sizes.
The atlas.json file lists every normalized cell rectangle, indexed from the top left.

Files:
- eyes_4x4_pink.png: imagegen master on bright pink.
- eyes_4x4_transparent.png: imagegen RGBA background extraction.
- eyes_4x4_pupil_mask.png: pupil selection data measured from the gray regions.

Tint only the selected pupil pixels:
  rgb = mix(art.rgb, chosenColor.rgb, pupilMask.r)
  alpha = art.a

Read the mask as linear / Non-Color data.
Use a white material base-color factor. Do not multiply the entire atlas by a tint.
The mask is black over the white sclera, white highlights, black outlines and backdrop.
Choosing RGB (0, 0, 0) gives black pupils while retaining the white highlights.
The source alpha is preserved separately from the pupil selection mask.

The original and new character models have not been replaced with these generated
styles. This is a separate atlas for review and integration.
