Implement the mechanics needed for the new card set.

Combat:
- Minions can attack other minions, both deal their power as damage
- Minions die when toughness reaches 0

Keywords:
- Tag list on minions for keywords (e.g. ranged)
- Ranged: no return damage from non-ranged attackers
- Remove keyword from a minion (lose range)

Targeting:
- All-minions targeting (not pick-one)
- Can't-be-targeted status with turn duration
- Friendly/enemy/any minion targeting (exists, extend)

Stat modification:
- Permanent +power/+toughness buffs on minions
- -power debuffs on minions

Ownership:
- Steal: change owner of a minion

Forced combat:
- Make two specific minions fight each other

Triggers:
- On-attack triggers (Trap)
- On-damage triggers (Poacher, Archery Instructor)
- On-death triggers (Swordmaster)
- On-draw triggers (Scholar)

Delayed effects:
- Effects that resolve on a future turn (Plan, Formation duration)

Hand manipulation:
- Discard from hand (player chooses)
- Steal a card from opponent's hand

Class levels:
- Per-player, per-class level tracking
- Off-class cost penalty: (3 - level) extra energy
- Level-up conditions tied to triggers
