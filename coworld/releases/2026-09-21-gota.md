# GotA weekly update

Changes since Thursday, September 17, 2026.

**A new GotA week is starting, and many changes are happening: everyone's bot needs an update!** Start from the updated baseline and review the [bot guide](../gota/guide.md) before resubmitting.

- BASIC now uses Bassy and Fixxy, with decimal arithmetic, fractional movement and spell coordinates, and bitwise Boolean operators that may require changes to existing bot logic.
- Matches start with alternating picks from a shared pool of ten heroes, with a ten-second deadline per pick and a separate battle clock.
- Abilities now start locked and require bots to spend skill points explicitly with `levelAbility`, including upgrades as heroes level up.
- Dead heroes can spend gold to buy back immediately, the baseline bot buys back whenever possible, and natural respawn time is capped at 60 seconds.
- Stackable Portal Scrolls teleport heroes near surviving allied towers after a vulnerable three-second channel and have a shared 60-second cooldown.
- Health and mana consumables now stack and offer gradual or instant recovery with cooldowns, purchases require your own keep, and your spawn rapidly restores health and mana.
- Each barracks now spawns three melee creeps and one ranged caster, while towers have doubled HP and damage and barracks retain 950 HP.
- Each creep provides one shared 15 XP pool, with 15% reserved for the nearby last hitter and the other 85% split among all nearby heroes, preserving fractional XP.
- Heroes, creeps, Zeus, and Hades use our generated characters and animations, with dead eyes, improved action-camera priorities, and fixed-direction lighting with quick day/night transitions.
- The replay extractor exposes typed combat, reward, death, and rejected-action events, while replay version 51 and the updated Coworld package keep the simulation, web player, examples, and documentation aligned.
