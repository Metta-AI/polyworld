Interesting approach, gives ideas, so, some ideas, still unbalanced:

Archer:
- Trap: incapacitate or kill creatures
- Decoy: reverts damage dealt the last turn
- Sniper Shot: deals damage to a creature
- Hail of Arrows: deals 1 damage to all creatures
- Positioning: can bypass armor or blocking (haven't implemented this block/taunt/armor mechanics yet)

Warrior:
- Formation: N creatures can't be target for M turns
- Swords: +N power to all creatures
- Shields: +N toughness to all creatures
- Duel: make two creatures face each other
- Footsoldier: 2/1 creature
- Tactician: small creature, -1 power to a target creature

Mage:
- Control: become the owner of a creature
- Plan: draw a card, draw a card next turn
- Study: draw 2 cards discard 2 cards
- Summon: big bad expensive creature
- Poacher: steal a card from hand when hits
