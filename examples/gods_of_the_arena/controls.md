# Human controls

In human play, commands always control your assigned hero. Your hero's health,
abilities and inventory remain visible when you inspect another unit.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left-click a unit or top-bar portrait | Inspect it without issuing an attack |
| Right-click ground / enemy | Move / chase and attack |
| A, then left-click ground / enemy | Attack-move / chase and attack |
| Hold Space | Center and follow your hero; release for free camera |
| Click your hero's portrait | Recenter the camera |
| Middle mouse drag / arrow keys | Pan the camera |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom |
| Q / W / E / R | Use an ability; right-click to confirm aimed abilities |
| Shift + Q / W / E / R | Learn or upgrade an ability |
| F / G | Use item slots 1 / 2; right-click to aim a portal scroll |
| B | Open / close the shop |
| Esc | Cancel targeting, or open / close the match menu |

Right-click cancels armed attack-move and performs the normal move or attack.
Inspecting a unit does not select it as an ability target: hotkeys use the unit
under the pointer and the existing ability targeting rules. Idle heroes still
automatically acquire nearby enemies.

These changes apply to human play, including networked matches. Spectator and
replay selection, camera controls, and the A/S/D mouse aliases stay available.
